RECENT TALK EXAMPLE

Own the conversation: How Designers Lead the AI Shift

20–25-minute stage talk

When the pressure to adopt AI reached my design teams at Cvent, I started by asking what we could do faster. As I experimented, I realized that learning the technology also gave me a stronger voice in decisions about how we build products. I share how I earned trust with engineering and product, helped teams balance speed and quality, and spoke up for the research and testing that protect good product decisions.

Audiences leave with a practical way to shape their next AI conversation: bring a workflow they’ve tested, explain what it speeds up, and name the evidence the team still needs before making a decision.

RECENT WORKSHOP EXAMPLE

The AI-Powered UX Process

Hands-on workshop · 90 minutes or tailored to your needs

Many designers feel pressure to use AI in their workflows but aren’t sure where to start. This hands-on workshop gives them practical guidance and a complete UX workflow, showing where AI can help from research and ideation through prototyping while keeping human judgment, research, and testing in the process.

I can offer it as a conference workshop or in-house training for companies that want their teams to build confidence and get more hands-on with AI. We can tailor the session to the available time, tools your team wants to explore, and number of participants.
