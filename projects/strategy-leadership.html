STRATEGY & LEADERSHIP

From moving parts to shared direction.

I help teams turn complex challenges into a direction people can understand, shape, and act on together.

How I approach it

Make the problem clear. Bring the right people into the conversation. Agree on priorities and give designers the context to make sound decisions.

What that looks like

  • Connect customer needs to product and business priorities.
  • Create visibility across design, product, and engineering.
  • Give teams clear decision boundaries and room to lead.
