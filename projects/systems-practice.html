SYSTEMS & PRACTICE

Consistency that makes room to grow.

I build shared systems that reduce friction while giving teams the flexibility to solve the problem in front of them.

How I approach it

A library is only part of the work. Shared ownership, clear contribution practices, and ongoing education help a design system become a team habit.

What that looks like

  • Build scalable components and intentional theming.
  • Define contribution and review practices with the team.
  • Teach the decisions behind the system, not only the rules.
