About Me
Core values:

People First is my core value. I strive to be an advocate for Human Beings in a world focused on Business, Profit & Technology.

Secret Sauce:

As a Design Leader, I create vision, processes and strategy to lead design teams to deliver efficient, engaging and intuitive products that users love.
I prioritize my teams' well-being so we can joyfully deliver quality work and meet our timelines. A balance of genuine care and efficient processes is my secret sauce as a leader.

Super Powers:

Abundance of Empathy & Creative Passion
Being constantly aware of the big picture while paying attention to fine details
Adaptability to change in technology, design tools & the world as a whole. When life gives me lemons, I make a tasty lemonade :)
Ability to simplify complex problems into digestible, actionable tasks.

Ikigai (Life purpose):

Teaching & mentoring is my ikigai, I am a Design instructor at Algonquin College, Ottawa and that recently inspired me to develop the gift and joy I find in teaching into a Youtube channel to make design knowledge Open Source to everyone seeking it.

Many hobbies:

I find great peace and joy on white sandy beaches, in Yoga and meditation, cuddling my children, singing and playing my guitar, painting and gardening.

Most important quality:

I pour my heart and soul into whatever I choose to do!
